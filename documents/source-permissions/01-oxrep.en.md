**Subject:** Terms of use for the OxREP databases — a request from Vestigian, an open atlas of the ancient world

Dear Professor Wilson, dear Professor Bowman,

I am writing on behalf of Vestigian (https://vestigian.org), a free and open atlas of the ancient world. It puts settlements, roads, fortifications, findspots and inscriptions on one map, and lets visitors record what they find when they go and look. It began from the openly licensed records of vici.org, carries no advertising and no tracking, runs on servers within the EU, and is non-commercial. Our own records are published under CC BY-SA, with the structured data under CC0.

The OxREP databases hold exactly what our map is missing: shipwrecks, mines and quarries, olive-oil and wine presses, water technology, and cities. We would very much like to draw them as a reference layer, each mark opening a short record with a link back to its entry in your database.

Your pages carry the line "Content: OXREP, 2005–2026" but no licence, and the page that would hold site-wide terms is behind a university login. Rather than assume anything, we are asking:

1. May we show records from these databases on the Vestigian map — name, type, date range and the coordinates as you publish them — with a link to the original entry?
2. On what terms, and in what form would you like OxREP to be cited?
3. May we keep a copy in our own database so that the map can serve it quickly, or would you prefer that we only link? If an export exists (we noticed that the quarries' coordinates are given in the PDF version), may we ask for it?
4. The quarries' coordinates are described as accurate "to within several kilometres". We would draw them as approximate and say so on the mark. Is there a wording you would prefer?

**What you can expect from us**

- Credit in the map's credit line while the layer is shown, a named entry in the map key, and in every info box OxREP's name, the terms you give and a link to the original entry.
- A place on our public data and licence page (https://vestigian.org/licence), in the words you prefer.
- Your data kept apart from our own records and left out of our open data export, so your terms remain your terms. We will not alter it; if we find a likely error we will send it to you rather than correct it silently.
- Nothing is used before you answer.

**More visibility, if you would like it**

Beyond that credit we would be glad to give OxREP a more prominent place: the layer could be named "Courtesy of the Oxford Roman Economy Project" with your mark beside it; a small framed note — a cartouche, in the manner of an old map — could appear in a corner of the map when the layer is switched on; and a partner page on our site could present OxREP in your own words. None of this is required, and the standard credit stands either way. I would be happy to send a short sketch of these options.

We would also be pleased to link to OxREP from that page, and would be grateful if you considered a link to Vestigian from your Links or Affiliated Projects page in return.

Thank you for your time, and for a body of work that the rest of us build on. I would be glad to answer any questions, or to show you how your records would look before anything goes live.

With kind regards,

Alex Lernbecher
