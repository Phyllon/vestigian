**Subject:** Reusing PAS records on an open map of the ancient world — a licence question and a question about access

Dear colleagues at the Portable Antiquities Scheme,

I am writing on behalf of Vestigian (https://vestigian.org), a free and open atlas of the ancient world. It puts settlements, roads, fortifications, findspots and inscriptions on one map, and lets visitors record what they find when they go and look. It began from the openly licensed records of vici.org, carries no advertising and no tracking, runs on servers within the EU, and is non-commercial. Our own records are published under CC BY-SA, with the structured data under CC0.

Few sources show where Roman coins turned up in Britain as well as the PAS database does. We would like to draw Roman-period finds — beginning with coins — as a reference layer on our map, each mark opening a short record with a link back to its PAS page.

Your terms say that the PAS licenses its work under a Creative Commons licence, naming CC BY 3.0 and, in the same paragraph, "Share-Alike". Because those are not the same licence, and because we would rather ask than assume, we have three questions:

1. **Licence.** Which licence applies to the records — CC BY 3.0, or a ShareAlike licence? And may we use the attribution "The Portable Antiquities Scheme / The Trustees of the British Museum", or would you prefer a different form?
2. **Access.** Our requests to finds.org.uk meet a bot challenge. Would you allow us to harvest public records through OAI-PMH at a low rate, identified by a user agent that names Vestigian and a contact address? If you would prefer a data dump, or to add us to an allow-list, we would follow whatever you advise.
3. **Precision and scope.** We would show a find-spot no more precisely than the public record itself does, name no finder and no landowner, and keep to Roman-period finds. Is there anything in how finds are presented on a map that you would want us to avoid, or records you would ask us to leave out?

**What you can expect from us**

- Credit in the map's credit line while the layer is shown, a named entry in the map key, and in every info box the PAS's name, the licence and a link to the original record.
- A place on our public data and licence page (https://vestigian.org/licence), in the words you prefer.
- Your records kept apart from our own and left out of our open data export, so your terms remain your terms. We will not alter them; if we find a likely error we will send it to you rather than correct it silently.
- Nothing is used before you answer.

**More visibility, if you would like it**

Beyond that credit we would be glad to give the PAS a more prominent place: the layer could be named "Courtesy of the Portable Antiquities Scheme" with your mark beside it; a small framed note — a cartouche, in the manner of an old map — could appear in a corner of the map when the layer is switched on; and a partner page on our site could present the Scheme in your own words, including how the public can take part. None of this is required, and the standard credit stands either way. I would be happy to send a short sketch of these options.

We would link to the PAS from that page, and would be grateful if you considered a link to Vestigian from your links or partners page in return.

Thank you for your time, and for a scheme that has changed what we know. I would be glad to answer any questions, or to show you how the records would look before anything goes live.

With kind regards,
