A "Dated survey sites" overlay: 4,671 sites whose Hellenistic-to-Byzantine period is read from the prose of eight sources inside the Ancient Sites and Monuments Survey (Jensine Andresen) — South Levant survey settlements, Syro-Palestinian chamber tombs, Marmarica, the LuwianSiteAtlas, Canmore, Historic England scheduled monuments, Sardinia's landscape plan and Cornwall's Romano-British enclosures. Destroyed and uncertain Cornwall records are kept and drawn faded. It is also searchable by name and exported as its own ODbL GeoJSON feed, `/api/survey-sites/sites.geojson`, kept apart from the CC BY-SA / CC0 site feeds; the Survey is listed on `/partners`. Run `pnpm db:migrate`, then `pnpm import:survey-sites` against `.import/ancient-monuments-survey-*.csv`. The survey is ODbL (share-alike); see `reference-overlays.md` before publishing.
