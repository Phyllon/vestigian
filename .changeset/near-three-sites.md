A switch in the new map settings (the gear at the bottom right) puts the featured sites around the view on the map, scrollable, on phones too — the compact list at most half the screen tall. The choice is remembered on the device. An enlarge button in the list's header ("Big picture") shows one site at a time with its photograph across the full width (about 40% of the screen) and, underneath, its period, place, a summary, how many photographs and endorsements it has, and the photograph's credit. The settings gather the featured switch and "every site as its own marker" into one control in place of two floating buttons, and the introduction's two steps for them became one. The featured endpoint now returns that extra detail for each site.
