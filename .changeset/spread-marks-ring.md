Sites whose markers lie on top of one another are now spread apart instead of piled: from zoom 9 they are drawn as a ring (up to eight) or a square grid of their own icons, each shrunk until they no longer cover one another, with a dot at the true place and a thin line to it. Zooming in lets them settle back where they belong. Tapping a small icon still lists everything under your finger.
