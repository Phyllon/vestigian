Reference marks' info boxes say more about the spot. Every mark that stands at a point (Barrington places, battles, ports, coin hoards, minor antiquities, inscriptions, mints, monuments, hillforts) now shows its coordinate, how far it is from you once you have used the locate button, and its source and licence. An "About this spot" section, fetched only when opened, adds the nearest town and country, the elevation (to a precision the tapped zoom supports), and the years the Roman state's recorded extent covered it, following the period scrubber's year when it is on. Adds `GET /api/spot`. Also fixes the Dutch "Wat" in year ranges, and makes the Coin Hoards credit link use https.
