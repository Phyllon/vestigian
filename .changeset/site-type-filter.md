Site types can be filtered. A new button in the bottom-right column (shown while "Sites" is on) opens a panel of vici's own kinds, grouped by category, with "All"/"None" per group and a count on the button. A chosen kind narrows the sites, their selection rings, labels and the matching tracings; nothing chosen shows everything. While a filter is held the sites are drawn from the every-site tiles, so a wide view loads more data. Not remembered between visits.
